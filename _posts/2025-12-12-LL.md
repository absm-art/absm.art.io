## blob post 12/12/2025

Well shit, I guess I kinda hope you never read this. Maybe if you do, it at least won’t make things worse.

It’s been a long time.

Months, years, decades of my life between today and these experiences that still somehow feel immediate, present, dangerous.

I wish I could thank you. What happened wasn’t how I wanted it to be, I’ll always take my share of the blame, however big. But, I also changed.

It’s not something that would ever make sense until you experience it for yourself, I guess. I felt a way I didn’t know was possible, even though nothing supernatural took place. But somehow, the tangible things that did happen made me feel so different in that moment, so new, so alive, that I forgot who I had been and how to be that person.

When I woke up, it was maybe the first time I truly realized I couldn’t go back to who I had been, that the past was really gone for good. It was a crashing, deafening sound, following me everywhere. I had been putting that pain into everything I did, and in the time after we met, I let fear of losing what I care about and fear of tarnishing who I am in the eyes of others be the only thing in my mind. 

I projected all of that fear and all of that torment onto things I wanted, people I cared about, just like I had done before. I projected it onto the way I interpreted your words and actions. I let it speak for me in ways I regret deeply. I’m sorry for the ultimatums and the wild mood swings, and I’m sorry for blaming you for my feelings.

But I also took that pain and the way it led me to be hurtful towards you, learned from it, and regrew a part of my body and mind that had died eons ago. I met new people, experienced new things, and found new sensations and new explanations for feelings and thoughts that have triggered these same painful and self-destructive overreactions all my life. I found new expression in the ways I have tried to channel my feelings, both feelings for you and for the many people I’ve met in the time before and since then. I’m happy to report that the very same personal character references from back then will all still vouch for me now, along with a whole new motley bunch of scallawags (and a co-captain I think you would adore).

I’ve failed a lot over the last two years, and in many material ways I haven’t progressed towards goals I had for myself from before we even started talking. But in the ways I’ve found to be meaningful, I’ve become a real human being. Maybe all of this is just to impress you personally, or win your heart despite all the dumb crap I said to you. I don’t really think that’s true or possible, but I think it’s a part of who I am that I can still dream about those things. I probably always will, and it will probably always be a part of what I make and say and do. You changed me. Thank you.

You remain my muse for no reason at all. It was wonderful to see you, in the truest sense of the word. My eyes have been glazed over for days, imagining the incredible things I’ve seen, and dreaming of all the things I want to show you.

Love,
Andy.
