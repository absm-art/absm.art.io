## Blob post 12/20/2025

Dear . . . . . . . . . . . . ,

I’m writing to you again. Yet, you remain in another land, leading another life, wearing a name I’ve tried to forget. I don’t expect this letter will reach you. I think I prefer it this way.

I’m beginning a new part of my journey.

I’ve spent the last few months woodworking nearly daily, centering myself around the goals and needs that I’m understanding for the first time, and building relationships and a place in my community and world that feels like home. I’ve been using digital art and making music as ways to express and focus the struggles I experience internally. I feel like I know myself now… but that has also resulted in me knowing who I have been, and what I have been through.

I have a lot of demons. Someday I’ll write about them, draw them, speak about them. I have a past full of mistakes, mistakes that hurt people and made stability impossible for me and people around me. 

For now, I’m making comics that detail the way I experience the world. My hope is that I can continue to make them, and stay in touch with the part of myself that is in turmoil almost all the time. But the time in my life when I let those demons make decisions for me and keep me from happiness is over. I’ll use this space to document my treatment, my experience with medication and therapy, and hopefully the ways I am able to construct a life for myself.

![Tuliptree Leaf]({{site.baseurl}}assets/images/IMG_0306.png)

I have a lot of shame about sharing my mental health struggles, even though it may not seem like it from the way I use social media. Especially with you, your judgment, your volatility. I’m a lot like you, though maybe more like your brother.

It’s taken a lot of time and growth for me to decide that it’s necessary, accountable, and healing for me to use the space and voice that I have to share how I have hurt people and the ways I am addressing those behaviors. I’m open about how I feel for others; I should be open about how I feel about myself. Hopefully this space can be healing, educational, and restorative to you.

I promise this won’t be the only content I post from now on, I want to grow in other ways as well. And someday this journey will change, or end, or become a different journey altogether. I’m learning to accept that, too.

Love,
Andy
