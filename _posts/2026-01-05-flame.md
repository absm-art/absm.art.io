## blob post 01/05/2026


It's likely schizophrenia, friends. Well, friend. Don't want to count people that aren't really there. 

I've made a mess of my social world. I think a couple of people with standing in places that I want to be welcome in think poorly of me and my behavior. I'm not sure how to rectify it. Paranoia and Grandeur both affect me badly to begin with, one reason I want to post publicly and make honest art regardless of the "quality" or consistency of what I make. It's hard to feel safe or invited in places I've been going for 20 years, and trying to break new ground socially has always been difficult at best. I struggle with crowds and thinking others are mocking me, or secretly dislike me. 

This post probably feels out of place, like it was written by someone else. I'm learning to recognize that I experience different modes of expression and awareness when I'm in different moods; I am having a complex social experience with someone I barely know, and it's making me feel a strange detachment from my social life. I really only decided to write this post as a way to document this sensation, this liminal awareness, my fingers and toes being slightly wrong, this place between sadness and fear.

I feel incoherent. I wish I could apologize enough to make things right, or to fix myself. I don't think there's a way to exist in the world that doesn't require me to do a lot of work, both personal and social, both penitence and growth.

wish me luck,
